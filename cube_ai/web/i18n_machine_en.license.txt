The generated English message catalog includes translations produced offline
with Argos Translate's Chinese–English model version 1.9. The original OPUS-MT
model is attributed to Jörg Tiedemann and Santhosh Thottingal, “OPUS-MT —
Building open translation services for the World”, EAMT 2020, and is licensed
under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Model package: https://argos-net.com/v1/translate-zh_en-1_9.argosmodel
The catalog also contains project-specific human corrections. No user content
is sent to a translation provider by the application.
